Methodology & Disclaimer

Archival Standard: The Bangladesh Islamophobia Archive operates as an independent, non-partisan empirical project documenting religious hostility, vilification, hate speech, and physical rights violations.

Corroboration: No incident is logged into the live ledger without verified open-source corroboration, primary multimedia authentication, or reporting from recognized news institutions.

Rectifications: Any cited individual or institution requesting factual correction or providing additional legal context may do so via our editorial contact.
