Methodology & Legal Disclaimer

Non-Partisan Archival Mandate: The Bangladesh Islamophobia Archive is an independent, non-partisan documentation effort dedicated to preserving public-interest records of religious discrimination, vilification, hate speech, and physical harm.

Verification Standard: Incidents are compiled from verified mainstream news reports, authenticated audiovisual documentation, and open-source intelligence. We do not endorse vigilante actions or extrajudicial rhetoric.

Right to Clarification: Individuals or institutions cited are entitled to submit corrections, context, or legal rectifications to the editorial team.
